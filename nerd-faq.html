📅 October 5, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✍️ NerdConnects Team

General Questions

NERD stands for the Nigeria Education Repository & Databank. It is a national digital archive established to collect, preserve, and provide access to academic research output from Nigerian higher education institutions. This includes theses, dissertations, project reports, and other academic documents.

Learn more in our detailed guide: What is NERD?

Yes, NERD registration is mandatory for most postgraduate students in Nigerian universities. It is typically a graduation requirement — your degree may not be fully processed without completing NERD registration. Some universities also require it for undergraduate final-year projects.

NERD registration is primarily for:

  • Postgraduate students — Master's (M.Sc, M.A, M.Ed, MBA, etc.) and Doctoral (Ph.D) students
  • Undergraduate students — In some institutions, B.Sc/B.A final-year projects also require submission
  • Researchers — Academic staff with publishable research output

If you've completed or are completing a thesis, dissertation, or final-year project at a Nigerian institution, you likely need NERD registration.

Document & Process Questions

You need the following documents:

  • Thesis/dissertation/project report (PDF format)
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Signed certification/approval page
  • Abstract of your thesis
  • Payment proof
  • Authorization letter (if required by your institution)

See our complete checklist: NERD Requirements

Your thesis must be in PDF format. It should be a complete, properly formatted document including the title page, abstract, all chapters, references, and appendices. The file should not exceed 50MB in size. Word documents (.docx) are not accepted — please convert to PDF before uploading.
