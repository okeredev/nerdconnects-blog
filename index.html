Avoid Delays

Common Reasons Theses Get Rejected

Over the years, we've seen hundreds of submissions get held up by avoidable issues. Keep an eye out for these:

❌ Unsigned or blurry supervisor approval page

The national repository requires a legible scanned copy of your signed approval page. If the signature is blurry, phone-scanned poorly, or unsigned, it gets rejected.

❌ File size exceeding 50 Megabytes

Large images, high-res charts, and raw datasets often push PDFs over the 50MB portal ceiling. The file must be compressed without losing text readability.

❌ Name discrepancy with school records

Your author name on the thesis must strictly match your official matriculation record. Mismatches trigger verification flags at your university's PG school.

❌ Remita payment generated under wrong service code

Generating a generic Remita RRR instead of the dedicated educational databank repository code means your payment won't link to your submission.
